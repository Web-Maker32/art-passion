Three-file leaf module with a clear separation of concerns:
- `lib/orders.ts` is the core business layer: it computes totals via `calculateShippingFee`, generates order numbers through an atomic `OrderCounter` row (`AP-{year}-{seq}`), and runs the full checkout inside a Prisma `$transaction` that locks the Cart row (`FOR UPDATE`), validates product status/stock (with advisory lock `pg_advisory_xact_lock(48201937)` for cross-process safety), decrements variant stock atomically, persists the order + items, and clears the cart. Duplicate submissions are handled by `idempotencyKey` pre-check plus P2002 catch.
- `lib/order-access.ts` is a self-contained security boundary: it builds a three-part cookie `orderNumber.token.signature`, signs it with HMAC-SHA256 over `env.ORDER_ACCESS_SECRET`, verifies signatures with `timingSafeEqual`, and exposes helpers to set/get the cookie and select a valid token from URL or cookie against a stored hash.
- `lib/order.ts` is a thin re-export shim exposing only `calculateOrderTotals` and `createOrderFromCart` to the rest of the app.
Dependency direction is one-way: orders → shipping/crypto/db; order-access → crypto/env/Next headers; order.ts → orders.ts only.
