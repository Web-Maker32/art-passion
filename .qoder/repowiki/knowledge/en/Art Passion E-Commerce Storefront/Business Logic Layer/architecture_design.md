The `lib/` directory is a flat server-only business layer consumed by Next.js route handlers. Cross-cutting concerns are centralized: `db.ts` exposes a singleton Prisma client backed by Neon (`PrismaNeon` adapter) with hot-reload guard via `globalThis.dbClient`, and `env.ts` validates every runtime secret/config through a single Zod schema that all children import. Domain modules split into two layers — pure domain models (`cart-domain.ts`, `product-filters.ts`) and server orchestration (`orders.ts`, `order-access.ts`, `shipping.ts`, `whatsapp.ts`, `crypto.ts`, `rate-limit.ts`) — while `payments/provider.ts` and `payments/manual.ts` abstract payment backends and `queries/categories.ts` / `queries/products.ts` wrap Prisma reads. Children communicate only through these shared primitives (prisma, env, crypto, phone, format); there is no internal package registry or event bus between siblings.
