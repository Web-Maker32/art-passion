Three-layer split: `app_routes` (Next.js App Router pages/layouts) renders UI and calls server actions/API routes; `business_logic` exposes typed functions (cart, orders, payments, shipping, product queries, WhatsApp/phone helpers) that own domain rules; `data_layer` owns the Prisma schema (`prisma/schema.prisma`) and client generation. Wiring is centralized at the root: `next.config.ts` declares Cloudinary/placehold.co image allow-lists and Turbopack Tailwind loader; `prisma7.config.ts` points Prisma at `DIRECT_URL` with seed script `tsx prisma/seed.ts`; `package.json` scripts (`db:generate`, `db:migrate`, `db:seed`, `db:studio`, `test`, `typecheck`) orchestrate the full stack. The data layer is the single source of truth — both business logic and routes consume the generated Prisma client rather than querying the database directly.
